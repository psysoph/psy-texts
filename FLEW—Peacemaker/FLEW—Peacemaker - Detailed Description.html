For FLEW ("Peacemaker"), the physical world and bodily comfort are not goals that must be won. They are the natural setting of life. Their First Physics works so smoothly and naturally that FLEW often does not even notice it. Because of this, they may wrongly think they are an easygoing 4F type. However, the true nature of First Physics becomes very clear during a crisis. When a person with 4F completely forgets about food, sleep, and basic needs, FLEW does the opposite. They return to the body. Good food, proper rest, and physical comfort become the strong base that helps them think clearly again and gives them the energy to act.

The "Peacemaker" has a wise kind of laziness. They strongly dislike pointless rushing and extra effort. FLEW takes from the material world exactly what is needed for personal comfort and easily removes everything unnecessary. This healthy saving of energy helps them stay calm inside and leaves a great amount of strength for the things they truly enjoy: talking with people, thinking, and doing useful work.

For this type, learning and thinking are not ways to prove that they are better than others. They are some of life's greatest pleasures. In the area of Logic, FLEW feels completely free, confident, and safe. They love drawing other people into thinking games, gently inviting them into discussions and honest exchanges of ideas.

At the same time, the "Peacemaker" has no aggression or stubborn belief in one fixed truth. They bring stubborn debaters back down to earth with light and harmless humor. With shy and unsure people, they do the opposite: they help them open up by patiently and kindly turning difficult theories into simple language that feels easy to understand.

When the "Peacemaker's" speech suddenly becomes too sharp, bitter, or full of cruel humor, it is almost always a warning sign. This sharp shield is used to protect their most sensitive area—Third Emotion. Because of it, FLEW often doubts whether their feelings are proper and becomes very sensitive to other people's reactions. They may often think someone is trying to hurt them, even when the other person meant nothing bad.

The "Peacemaker's" inner thoughts are often filled with quiet and tiring questions: “Do they need me?” “Do I seem too pushy?” “How do they see me?” When alone, they may feel shy about their own smile, facial expressions, a voice they think sounds “dull,” or a laugh that seems unnatural to them. FLEW deeply needs complete acceptance and a warm, safe atmosphere where they do not have to pretend.

The "Peacemaker" is strongly drawn to people who can gently warm up and free their closed emotions. They become deeply attached to such people. When the human world becomes too tiring, FLEW finds peace in nature. They have a remarkably finely tuned sensitivity toward plants and animals, intuitively understanding their unspoken needs, and it is through this quiet care that they regain their lost inner balance.

Fourth Will makes the "Peacemaker" a very pleasant, polite, and fair companion.

They honestly want everyone around them to feel comfortable. Because of this, they consider other people's wishes and try not to hurt anyone by accident. FLEW has no class-based pride and does not flatter powerful people. They speak with the same calm respect to the head of a large company and to an ordinary cleaner, because they believe all people are equal and free from the start. The other side of this softness is difficulty with major planning. FLEW may find it hard to create large long-term goals on their own or find a blind inner drive to fight for them. To move forward, they often need to use their stronger functions in a clever way. They look for an interesting idea in the goal or a clear material benefit.

The main direction of the FLEW personality is an unusual mix of quiet love for life and great inner strength. Yes, they may be slightly sad at times and prefer to wait and watch in a new place. But behind this outer passiveness is a deeply sensitive, thoughtful, fair, and truly free person who knows how to value life itself.
