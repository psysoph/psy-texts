1st Physics (1F): "Owner"

The first position makes the physical world the main priority for FLEW. They understand life through the body, usefulness, and comfort.

  • Selfishness in everyday comfort: FLEW clearly understands the needs of their body and will not accept discomfort just to be polite. They have a strong sense of personal space. They carefully protect their belongings and strongly dislike it when someone moves their furniture or changes their daily habits without permission.
  • Sensible use of resources: They know how to save money, gain real practical value, and create a comfortable home. They often have good taste and enjoy quality clothes, tasty food, and comfort, but they choose these things for themselves, without showing off.
  • Focus on results: When it comes to health, everyday tasks, and physical problems, FLEW cares about the final result, not long discussions. If they need to solve a household problem or treat an illness, they quietly use a trusted method and fix the problem.

2nd Logic (2L): "Mentor"

The second position controls thinking and communication. For FLEW, Logic is both a useful tool and an enjoyable activity, not a way to prove that they are better than others.

  • Flexible thinking: FLEW loves debating, discussing theories, and studying information. For them, it is a light game, not a battle over who is right. They are not stubborn about ideas, listen calmly to other views, and can change their mind when they hear strong arguments.
  • Explaining knowledge: They know how to explain difficult things in simple words. Because of this gift, FLEW can become excellent teachers, scientists, editors, and analysts who can turn any confusing idea into something clear.
  • Intellectual help: People of this type are always ready to help others understand a difficult subject without asking for anything in return. They freely share what they know and spend time guiding others toward a clear answer.

3rd Emotion (3E): "Wallflower"

The third position is the main area of inner conflict and insecurity for FLEW. Their emotional world is private and sensitive.

  • Emotional restraint: FLEW often looks cold or dry because it is hard for them to show strong feelings first, hug someone, or say “I love you.” At the same time, they are deeply afraid of emotional pressure, fake drama, and loud emotional scenes. When people shout at them, they usually close off at once.
  • Hidden sensitivity: Inside, FLEW may feel things deeply and may truly love art or poetry. However, they show this soft and emotional side very rarely and only to the closest people they have trusted for a long time.
  • Need for gentleness: People of this type need a calm, steady, and accepting atmosphere. They look for people who do not create mood swings or drama and who respect their need for inner peace.

4th Will (4W): "Follower"

The fourth position makes FLEW very easygoing in matters of power, ambition, and control.

  • Easygoing nature: FLEW rarely wants to lead and often follows other people’s plans, as long as those plans do not harm their physical comfort. If someone they respect suggests a trip or activity, they will usually pack and go without long discussion.
  • Avoiding responsibility: People of this type prefer someone else to make major life decisions, such as where to study, where to live, or how to grow a business. This saves them from carrying too much responsibility.
  • Peaceful nature: They strongly dislike open fights over power, status, or control. If someone puts strong pressure on FLEW, they are more likely to leave than waste energy fighting back.

Overall Psychological Portrait

FLEW ("Peacemaker") is a grounded, sensible, and peaceful person who knows how to live in harmony with themselves and the world around them. They have a bright and flexible mind and enjoy useful discussions while also standing firmly in the real world and skillfully creating comfort, stability, and a pleasant home. In communication, FLEW is easygoing and readily follows other people’s plans. However, they keep a strong distance in emotional matters, hiding a soft and sensitive inner world behind an outwardly cold appearance.
