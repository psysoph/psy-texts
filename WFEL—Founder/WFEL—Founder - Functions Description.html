1st Will (1W): "Ruler"

The first function makes Will very strong, dominant, and completely confident.

  • Determination and responsibility: WFEL naturally dislikes being controlled. They see the goal, ignore obstacles, and willingly take responsibility for themselves and for the people around them.
  • Leadership: They make decisions on their own and usually do not expect them to be debated. WFEL believes they know the best way to do things and expects others to follow.
  • View of power: They see power as a natural tool for keeping order, not as a game. Because of this, they often ignore other people's opinions or wishes.

2nd Physics (2F): "Artisan"

The second function makes Physics a place of confidence, balance, and healthy interaction with the physical world.

  • Practical and hardworking: These are active, tough, and practical people. They enjoy working with their hands, building or fixing things, earning money, and solving everyday problems.
  • Helpful and supportive: When it comes to home life, health, or money, WFEL enjoys working together with others. They are happy to help a friend with home repairs, give useful advice about health or appearance, or explain how to manage money wisely.
  • Relationship with the physical world: They understand their body well, enjoy good food, sports, and physical work. The physical world feels safe, familiar, and comfortable to them.

3rd Emotion (3E): "Wallflower"

The third function is the most sensitive part of the personality. It is where the biggest fears and hidden wishes are found.

  • Emotionally reserved: WFEL feels emotions very deeply but rarely shows them. They often seem cold, tough, or emotionless, even though they care a lot inside. Showing love, joy, or sympathy naturally can be difficult, so they keep their feelings hidden.
  • Fears and sensitivity: They are very uncomfortable around emotional outbursts, dramatic scenes, emotional manipulation, or public embarrassment. To protect this weak spot, they often appear serious, quiet, or distant.
  • What they need: Deep inside, WFEL wants calm, honest, and steady emotional support. They want to feel loved simply for who they are, without being expected to make big romantic speeches or dramatic displays of emotion.

4th Logic (4L): "Disciple"

The fourth function means that abstract debates and complicated theories are not very important.

  • Practical thinking: The "Founder" does not enjoy long discussions, philosophy, or arguing over obvious things. An idea only matters if it can be used right now to solve a real problem or reach a real goal.
  • Learning style: They easily trust experienced people and prefer clear instructions that already work. If a system gets good results, WFEL usually sees no reason to spend time learning every detail behind it.
  • Communication style: They speak briefly and clearly and stay on the subject. Long, abstract conversations quickly become tiring, so they naturally guide discussions back to practical matters.

Overall Psychological Portrait

WFEL ("Founder") is like a solid rock. They combine an iron will, practical thinking, and calm emotional control. They are natural leaders and excellent crisis managers who quietly take responsibility, bring order, and build a strong material foundation for themselves and the people they care about. Behind their serious, practical appearance and dislike of abstract discussions is a deeply sensitive person who quietly longs for gentle acceptance, emotional warmth, and peaceful relationships.
