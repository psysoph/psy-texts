WFEL ("Founder") is a type with strong inner magnetism and a complex personality. They combine high social energy, natural leadership, and a very private inner world. They can lead many people while still keeping everyone at a distance and letting very few people truly know them.

When the "Founder" sets a goal, they are almost impossible to stop. They push through every obstacle and see every challenge as a test of their strength. But if outside circumstances completely block their plans, WFEL takes it very hard. When their plans fall apart, they become quiet and serious and withdraw into themselves for a long time. In this state, they become completely independent. It is almost impossible to change their mind or motivate them from the outside. WFEL does not look for pity or emotional support. They believe their goals and battles are their own responsibility. They usually ignore criticism, but if someone pushes them too hard, they may answer with sudden anger.

WFEL is active and energetic. Sitting still for too long is difficult for them. Their hobbies are usually connected to the physical world, such as sports, martial arts, building things, or working with their hands. During serious conflicts, the "Founder" can respond with great strength and determination.

When it comes to appearance, WFEL believes it is a personal choice. Some people of this type build the perfect body through bodybuilding or body modifications. Others choose a simple, practical, and neat style. Whatever they choose, they do it for themselves, not for other people's approval. WFEL is usually free from worrying about what others think of their appearance.

Their most sensitive area is expressing emotions freely. Because they control themselves so much, their attempts to open up can seem awkward or stiff. Their laugh may sound forced, their facial expressions may seem sharp, and their gestures may become sudden. The "Founder" truly relaxes only around trusted people, where they no longer feel the need to control every emotion. In that safe space, they become very different. They laugh freely, show anger honestly, and are not afraid to be vulnerable.

Deep inside, WFEL secretly enjoys romance, flirting, playful hints, and teasing. However, they usually do not know how to start these things themselves. They hope their partner will take the first emotional step. Instead, they express love through their strong Second Physics (2F). Their love looks like warm hugs, taking care of your health, buying thoughtful gifts, or cooking a special dinner. It is much easier for them to prove love through actions than through beautiful words, although this becomes easier as the relationship grows deeper.

The "Founder" does not enjoy long discussions that go nowhere. Once they have made up their mind, that decision becomes solid. Changing it with logical arguments alone is almost impossible. Part of the reason is that WFEL honestly sees little value in endless debates. To them, abstract ideas matter much less than real actions. This way of thinking, together with their powerful First Will (1W), makes them even more stubborn and difficult to influence. Convincing them that their big goals are unrealistic is almost impossible.

The only thing that can truly change WFEL's plans is deep personal love. When the "Founder" truly falls in love, their armor comes down. They become willing to do almost anything for the person they love and will work incredibly hard to build a happy and comfortable life together. But this generosity lasts only while they feel that their love is returned. If they feel ignored, lied to, or rejected, they close their heart immediately. By nature, this type is also very possessive. They become deeply jealous, and if they are betrayed, their anger can be very dangerous.

In the end, WFEL is a determined, dominant, and strong leader who, despite their independence, deeply needs emotional warmth, peace, and kindness. Behind their tough appearance is someone who struggles with deep loneliness. When left alone with their thoughts for too long, they often find it difficult to deal with painful emotions by themselves. Warm, genuine contact with people they love helps them find emotional balance and use their great physical energy for building rather than fighting.

Because of this, the most important path of growth for the "Founder" is learning better social skills, becoming more flexible, and developing emotional intelligence. This helps soften the sharp edges of their personality and allows them to become a truly wise and respected leader who reaches great goals while staying in harmony with the people around them.
