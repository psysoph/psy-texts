EWLF (“Muse”) is a type that brings together a deep inner world of feelings and a strong sense of personal duty. Unlike some other people with First Emotion, the “Muse” does not drown others in their feelings or force everyone to share the same mood. Their empathy is mature and useful. They truly care about people, but they show it not through empty tears, but through real support and motivation. They do not like endless complaining or pity that does not help a person grow. Their emotional presence is so gentle and calm that even very sensitive people feel comfortable beside them.

By nature, the “Muse” is a true giver. They react strongly to other people’s pain, know how to give confidence to those who doubt themselves, and gently push people toward action. However, the other side of this strong sense of duty is taking on too much responsibility and feeling too much guilt. Because of this, EWLF often mistakes a basic need for rest for laziness and forces themselves to work beyond their limits. They expect the same effort from close people. Excuses such as “I am not in the mood” or “I am depressed” do not seem like a good reason to do nothing. In teamwork, people can always rely on them. The “Muse” will not only complete their own part perfectly but may also quietly finish the work of slower coworkers in order to save the whole project.

The world of ideas is an endless source of joy for EWLF. Their great curiosity pushes them to explore very different areas of science and life. They may take on a very difficult project for free if the process itself feels interesting or if they truly want to understand how a complex system works from the inside. At the same time, the material world is much less important to them. Fourth Physics makes them uninterested in luxury, showing off status, or living only for pleasure. With a healthy and simple lifestyle, the “Muse” can live with a small number of things and little money while focusing on spiritual and intellectual values.

Among all people with Second Will, the “Muse” may be one of the most honest and responsible. They are natural organizers who work very well when they have freedom and can manage themselves. In communication, they are easygoing, fair, and free from arrogance. They build every relationship—marriage, friendship, or work—on equality, mutual help, and shared interest. They cannot stand strict ranks, pressure, or attempts to turn them into silent workers with no voice. For EWLF, a boss, teacher, or client is first of all an equal partner in a shared task. Deep inside, they are free from other people’s expectations. The only thing that truly controls them is their own strong code of honor.
