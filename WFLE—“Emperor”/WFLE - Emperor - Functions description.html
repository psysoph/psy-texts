1st Will (1W): “Ruler”

The first function forms the core of the WFLE personality. Will is very strong, self-focused, and solid here.

  • Self-Confidence: From childhood, WFLE knows what they want and truly believes they were born to manage people or important processes.
  • Leadership Style: Their leadership is strict but natural. They do not need to shout to make people listen. Others can feel their authority without words.
  • Attitude Toward Obstacles: Obstacles do not frighten them. Instead, difficulties make them even more determined. “I see the goal and ignore the obstacles” is the main motto of WFLE.

2nd Physics (2F): “Artisan”

The material world is WFLE’s natural area. They feel highly confident and comfortable in it.

  • Working With the Material World: WFLE has strong physical endurance. They enjoy working with their hands, improving spaces, earning money, and taking care of health.
  • Helping Others: Unlike First Physics, which often keeps resources for itself, the Second Physics of WFLE is ready to create and provide for others. They gladly cook for people, help with repairs, and give useful advice about business or everyday life.
  • Relationship With the Body: They understand their physical needs very well. They value good food, exercise, and comfort but do not turn these things into an obsession. They simply know how to manage them well.

3rd Logic (3L): “Skeptic”

This is the most sensitive, painful, and at the same time most interesting area for WFLE.

  • Intellectual Insecurity: WFLE often doubts whether they are correct, intelligent, well-read, or able to think logically. They react very painfully when someone makes them look foolish or catches them making a mistake during a discussion.
  • Argument Style: They enjoy long debates and careful searches for the truth. However, they may begin arguing only to prove they are right and protect their self-respect. Abstract theories with no practical use are often difficult for them because of the influence of Second Physics.
  • Defense Method: They may hide their insecurity behind the authority of First Will: “I said so, so it is correct.” They may also collect diplomas, certificates, and titles to prove their intelligence to the world.

4th Emotion (4E): “Spectator”

Pure feelings, strong excitement, and emotional sharing seem less important and difficult to understand for WFLE.

  • Emotional Coldness: WFLE does not usually show many emotions on their own. Their face and reactions may seem controlled, serious, or stone-like.
  • Emotional Influence: They strongly depend on the emotional atmosphere around them. When a cheerful person is nearby, WFLE becomes more open and lively. When someone is having a loud emotional breakdown, they often shut down or leave because they do not know how to handle it.
  • Art and Feelings: They often see art as something useful or as a way to relax. In love, they prefer to prove their feelings through actions, such as gifts, protection, and practical help through Second Physics, rather than through beautiful words.

Overall Psychological Portrait

WFLE (“Emperor”) is a natural leader with iron Will, great confidence, and strong practical thinking. They skillfully manage the material world, take responsibility for close people, and prove love through real actions rather than words. Behind their royal and emotionally controlled calmness lies a painful sensitivity to criticism of their intelligence. Because of this, they can become extremely stubborn in arguments.
