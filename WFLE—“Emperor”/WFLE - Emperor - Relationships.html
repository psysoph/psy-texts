In friendship and romantic relationships, WFLE (“Emperor”) is a stone wall, a safe base, and a powerful protector. They may not know how to speak beautifully about feelings, but they prove their loyalty through large and real actions.

Friendship

WFLE are very loyal but demanding friends who divide the world into “their people” and “outsiders.”

  • Protection: When WFLE accepts you as one of their people, you are strongly protected. During a serious problem, they will arrive in the middle of the night, find money, use their contacts, and pull you out of almost any crisis.
  • Directness and Honesty: They do not flatter people or avoid the truth. When a friend does something foolish, WFLE says it directly. They expect the same honesty from others.
  • Leader of the Group: In a group of friends, WFLE usually takes control without being officially chosen. They decide where everyone will go, which date to book, and who will handle each task. Arguing with them about planning is usually pointless.
  • No Forgiveness for Betrayal: WFLE may forgive weakness, foolish behavior, or sudden emotional actions. However, they will never forgive cheating, betrayal, or an attempt to damage their authority. They remove a traitor from their life forever and without mercy.

Love and Marriage

In love, WFLE follows the rule: "I came, I saw, I conquered." They dislike uncertainty and long chains of hints.

  • Leadership and Protection: WFLE quickly takes the role of head of the family, no matter their gender. They take full responsibility for money, safety, and the partner’s comfort. Beside them, the partner often stops worrying about everyday practical problems.
  • Love Through Action: It is difficult to get songs under the window or long love letters from WFLE because of Fourth Emotion. Instead, they show love by buying a car, planning the best vacation, solving health problems, or paying for education.
  • Loyalty and Reliability: WFLE prefers long and stable relationships. Once they choose someone, they remain loyal and build a strong, safe space around the family.
  • Support During Crises: During difficult moments in the partner’s life, WFLE does not panic. They stay very calm, take the main pressure on themselves, quickly create a plan, and give the partner a strong sense of safety.

Main Relationship Challenges

  • Emotional Blindness: Because of Fourth Emotion, WFLE often overlooks the partner’s inner pain, hints, and romantic needs. When the partner complains about a lack of warmth, WFLE may honestly feel confused. They believe that money, protection, and practical care should already be more than enough.
  • Extreme Control and Authoritarian Behavior: First Will pushes the “Emperor” to make all major family decisions alone. Without noticing it, they may weaken the partner’s Will, demand complete obedience, and punish any attempt to question their authority.
  • Intellectual Aggression in Daily Life: When someone criticizes their views or practical decisions, WFLE quickly protects sensitive Third Logic. Instead of a calm talk, they may begin a long and angry argument simply to win. They may also end the discussion by saying, "I said so, so it is correct.”
  • Jealousy of Other People’s Authority: WFLE is naturally possessive. They react very painfully when the partner asks other people for advice, support, or expert opinions, especially someone of another gender. The “Emperor” may see this as doubt about their strength or intelligence. This hits sensitive Third Logic and can lead to jealousy and heavy control.

Compatibility With Other Types

ELFW (“Dramatist”)—Balanced Relationship. This is a deeply harmonious relationship based on perfect mutual support. The gentle and sensitive “Dramatist” fills the “Emperor’s” life with warm and honest emotions, helping them relax without pressure. In return, the strong “Emperor” gladly takes responsibility, protects the partner from life’s difficulties, and handles the material side of life. They safely cover each other’s weak areas and can build a very strong bond.

WLFE (“Revolutionary”)—Growth-Oriented Relationship. This is a powerful relationship between like-minded people and strong leaders who share a practical view of life. The “Revolutionary” helps the “Emperor” organize thoughts and gently reduce fears about intelligence. In return, the “Emperor” creates comfort and stability in the physical world. Their main challenge is learning to give way in the fight for leadership because both have dominant First Will.

LEWF (“Philosopher”)—Dramatic Relationship. This is a relationship of opposites that can quickly turn into a battlefield. The practical and authoritarian “Emperor” naturally pressures the “Philosopher’s” sensitive Will and becomes annoyed by their lack of practical action. The “Philosopher” then closes off and answers with intellectual pride, hurting the “Emperor’s” hidden insecurities. The two partners speak very different emotional and mental languages and may repeatedly hurt each other’s most sensitive areas.
