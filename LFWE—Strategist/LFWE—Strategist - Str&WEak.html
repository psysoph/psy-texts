Strengths:

  • Powerful intelligence: Thinks clearly, organizes information well, and quickly finds the heart of difficult problems.
  • Practical and reliable: Handles everyday life well, earns money wisely, and creates comfort and stability.
  • Calm under pressure: Does not panic during crises and keeps a clear mind when others lose control.
  • Hardworking: Has strong physical endurance, finishes practical tasks, and enjoys helping people through actions.
  • Keeps promises: Takes responsibility seriously, keeps their word, and is someone family and coworkers can always depend on.

Weaknesses:

  • Stubborn and rigid: Can become too certain they are right, rejects other viewpoints, and rarely admits mistakes.
  • Emotionally distant: Often seems cold or quiet, struggles to express feelings, and finds emotional support difficult.
  • Indecisive and anxious: Constantly questions their own decisions and may delay important choices because they fear failure.
  • Sensitive pride: Takes criticism personally, hides ambitions, and may quietly resist people who hurt their pride.
  • Sensitive to other people's emotions: Quickly becomes exhausted by emotional drama, loud arguments, and constant conflict.
