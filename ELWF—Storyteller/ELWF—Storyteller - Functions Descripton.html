1st Emotion (1E): "Romantic"

First Emotion is the center of the ELWF personality. This person sees the world through feelings, images, and moods.

  • Very Strong Feelings: The feelings of Storyteller are powerful, deep, and often sad or tragic. They quickly notice lies, beauty, and the emotional power of art.
  • Emotional Dominance: Their emotions are either kept deep inside or shown openly without checking whether other people are ready for them. If they feel bad, everyone will know. If they become excited about something, their joy can spread to others.
  • Creative Talent: Many ELWF people become writers, poets, musicians, or actors. They can express inner struggles in a brilliant way, but they often make suffering seem beautiful or romantic.

2nd Logic (2L): "Mentor"

Second Logic is the area of confidence, open discussion, and helping other people understand things.

  • Flexible Thinking: Storyteller has a strong and flexible mind. They enjoy discussions, puzzles, searching for truth, and talking about deep ideas.
  • Teaching Others: Unlike First Logic, which often gives a final answer, Second Logic enjoys the process of explaining. ELWF is patient in debates, listens to other opinions, and gently leads the other person toward a logical answer.
  • Logic as a Tool for Emotion: Logic gives shape to their powerful feelings. ELWF can turn difficult inner emotions into clear, beautiful, and understandable ideas and words.

3rd Will (3W): "Rebel"

Third Will is the main weak point and the source of this type's inner pain.

  • Feeling Unrecognized: ELWF often doubts their strength, rights, and importance in society. They may feel that people do not value them, try to control them, or simply ignore them.
  • Conflicting Behavior: Their lack of confidence can appear in two very different ways. Sometimes they act overly obedient and say, "I do not want anything." At other times, they strongly and angrily defend their boundaries because they believe someone is taking away their rights.
  • Difficulty Making Choices: Storyteller finds major life decisions and long-term responsibility extremely difficult. They may spend years unable to choose. They need gentle support, but direct orders often make them resist.

4th Physics (4F): "Ascetic"

Fourth Physics is the area they care about the least. The physical and material world is usually a low priority for ELWF.

  • Little Interest in Everyday Comfort: Storyteller can live in simple or uncomfortable conditions without much complaint. They care little about luxury, expensive food, or famous brands. They may even forget to eat because they are lost in a book, an idea, or their own feelings.
  • Following Others in Practical Matters: In questions of health, household tasks, and money, they often depend on a partner. It is easier for them when someone else decides what they should wear, how they should treat an illness, or where they should work.
  • The Body as a Burden: They may see physical needs as something unimportant that gets in the way of feelings, ideas, and inner life.

Overall Psychological Portrait

ELWF ("Storyteller") is a deep, intelligent, and sensitive person who lives in a world of strong feelings, images, and meanings. They have deep empathy and a flexible mind that helps them turn difficult emotional experiences into beautiful and clear ideas. At the same time, they often suffer from self-doubt, low confidence, and a strong fear of responsibility. They care very little about money, comfort, or everyday tasks and deeply need a strong partner who can become a safe and dependable source of support.
