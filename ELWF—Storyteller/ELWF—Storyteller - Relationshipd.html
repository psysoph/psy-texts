In friendship and love, ELWF ("Storyteller") is one of the most loyal and emotionally deep partners but also one of the most difficult emotionally. They do not simply want to spend time together. They want a deep meeting of souls and complete acceptance.

Friendship

For friends, ELWF is a sensitive and understanding person who is perfect for deep late-night talks and rarely judges anyone.

  • Talks Until Sunrise: Friendship with ELWF is built on sharing thoughts and feelings. They dislike empty talk about the weather, but they can become the best listener for your secrets, deep questions, and emotional struggles.
  • Complete Acceptance: Because of their strong empathy, Storyteller does not judge a friend for weakness or mistakes. They understand, comfort, and help explain the situation step by step.
  • Passive About Staying in Touch: Because of vulnerable Third Will, ELWF rarely reaches out first. They may go a long time without calling because they are afraid of seeming annoying, even though they secretly hope you will contact them.
  • Distant From Practical Help: They probably will not help you move furniture or repair your car, but they will always support you emotionally.

Love and Marriage

ELWF is a loyal and romantic partner who gives deep feelings and looks for strong emotional closeness.

  • Waiting for a Fairy Tale: ELWF often idealizes the partner and makes the relationship seem more romantic than it really is. They want beautiful and deep love like the kind found in books, and ordinary daily life can be hard for them to accept.
  • Emotional Ups and Downs: Their First Emotion needs expression. A relationship with ELWF may include periods of very gentle happiness but also sadness, dramatic feelings, or sudden hurt. Life with them is rarely boring, though it can be emotionally tiring.
  • Need for a Strong Protector: ELWF looks for a partner who will handle practical problems and confidently guide the relationship. If the partner is also unsure and emotionally weak, ELWF can quickly become anxious.
  • Fear of Rejection: Because of vulnerable Will, they are deeply afraid of being abandoned or misunderstood. Even one cold comment from a partner may feel like a disaster. This can lead them to test the partner's love and loyalty.

Main Relationship Challenges

  • Emotional Ups and Downs: Because of powerful and impulsive First Emotion, ELWF may move from idealizing the partner and showing complete tenderness to sadness, drama, and tears without a clear outside reason.
  • Hidden Hurt: Because of vulnerable Third Will, they may stay silent for a long time about what hurts them because they fear open conflict. In the end, they may suddenly explode or end the relationship forever.
  • Avoiding Responsibility: They often avoid important life decisions and practical problems. Instead, they may spend a lot of time talking about what “should be done” without actually doing it.
  • Using Guilt: Because ELWF finds it difficult to clearly and confidently state personal needs, they may unknowingly take the role of a victim. Instead of speaking openly, they may show strong suffering, cry, or become cold and silent. This makes the partner feel guilty and forces them to guess what caused the hurt.

Compatibility with Other Types

FWLE ("Baron")—Balanced Relationship. This is an ideal and highly balanced relationship in which the partners perfectly protect each other's weak areas. Practical and dependable Baron takes care of the material side of life, saving Storyteller from frightening daily choices and financial worries. Their relationship is filled with deep respect. The strong Will of Baron gently guides and protects the vulnerable Will of Storyteller, while their deep talks and emotional connection give both partners a strong feeling of safety and comfort.

EWLF ("Muse")—Growth Relationship. This is a deep intellectual and emotional bond. The partners easily understand one another's thoughts and share a similar emotional world. They often understand each other's feelings without many words and create a safe space where fears and insecurities can slowly heal. However, this relationship has a serious weakness. Both partners are highly impractical, which can lead to total household disorder and financial chaos.

WFEL ("Founder")—Dramatic Relationship. This is a difficult relationship filled with powerful attraction and repeated painful clashes. The strong and controlling Will of Founder feels like cruelty and harsh pressure to the sensitive Storyteller. At the same time, the strong emotional ups and downs of Storyteller deeply annoy the partner. In this relationship, one person constantly demands practical discipline that the other cannot easily give. The other protects themself through emotional distance and stubborn intellectual arguments. This can leave both partners completely exhausted.
