Strengths:

  • Material Stability: They are skilled at earning money, creating comfort, and maintaining a stable household.
  • Exceptional Reliability: They remain calm under pressure, keep their promises, and provide dependable support for loved ones.
  • Wise Leadership: They lead without intimidation, respect personal boundaries, and excel at finding practical compromises.
  • Realism: They stay grounded, avoid unrealistic fantasies, and focus on concrete, achievable results.

Weaknesses:

  • Emotional Reserve: They often appear emotionally distant, conceal their feelings, and may struggle to express empathy openly.
  • Conservative Habits: They become attached to familiar routines, personal belongings, and established ways of living, making change difficult.
  • Intellectual Insecurity: Criticism of their thinking can affect them deeply, leading to excessive debating or pedantic behavior.
  • Emotional Suggestibility: Although emotionally restrained, they easily absorb other people's moods and can become vulnerable to emotional manipulation.
