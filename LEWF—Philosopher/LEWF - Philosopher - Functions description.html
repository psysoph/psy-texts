1st Logic (1L): "Thinker"

The first function shapes the size and depth of the personality. For LEWF, the world is something that must be understood.

  • Complete Confidence: LEWF has clear and organized thinking. Their own opinion comes first for them. They rarely doubt their conclusions.
  • Focus on Results: They want a finished intellectual result, such as a truth, idea, system, or clear plan. They do not enjoy long and empty arguments just for the sake of arguing. Once they believe they fully understand something, they see the subject as finished.
  • Communication Style: They speak with confidence, use few words, and often express ideas in short and memorable phrases. During intellectual discussions, they may seem proud or cold because they quickly notice mistakes in other people's thinking.

2nd Emotion (2E): "Actor"

The second function is an area of confidence, creativity, and open contact with the world.

  • Emotional Radar: LEWF notices the moods of other people very clearly. They know how to care, give gentle support, and share another person's pain or joy.
  • Managing the Atmosphere: They have many different emotional tones. They know how to joke, make a tense situation calmer, and act in a lively and expressive way. Unlike First Emotion, Second Emotion does not force its own feelings onto others. It adjusts to the person and the situation.
  • Creativity: LEWF often finds a place in art, poetry, writing, or psychology. Their intelligence, or 1L, is always closely connected with the world of feelings, or 2E.

3rd Will (3W): "Rebel"

The third function is LEWF's main sensitive area and the source of many inner struggles and doubts.

  • Feeling Like a Fake: LEWF often doubts their own strength, rights, talents, and place in society. Making decisions and taking serious responsibility can be very hard for them.
  • Sensitive Ego: They react painfully to criticism, orders, and pressure. At the same time, they may become moody, easily hurt, or jealous of another person's success.
  • Hidden Ambition: LEWF wants respect and recognition but is afraid to speak openly about their wishes. This creates an inner conflict: “I deserve more, but the world does not value me."

4th Physics (4F): "Ascetic"

The fourth function is an area of low interest. LEWF does not want to spend much mental energy on it.

  • Simple Everyday Life: LEWF can easily live with little comfort. They are not very demanding about food, clothes, or living conditions. They may forget to eat because they are deeply focused on a book or their thoughts.
  • Following Others in Material Matters: When it comes to health, money, and sexuality, LEWF often prefers to trust a partner. Life feels easier when someone else decides what to buy, where to live, or how to treat an illness.
  • Distance From the Body: They often see the body as a kind of outer shell for the mind and soul. Physical work is difficult for them and usually gives them little pleasure.

Overall Psychological Portrait

LEWF ("Philosopher") is a deep and refined intellectual who combines a sharp and organized mind with a very strong ability to understand other people's feelings. They live in a world of ideas, thought, and quiet observation. They need little in everyday life and care very little about luxury or expensive things. At the same time, behind their gentle appearance is a sensitive and self-doubting person who is deeply afraid of harsh pressure, criticism, and hard struggles in life.
