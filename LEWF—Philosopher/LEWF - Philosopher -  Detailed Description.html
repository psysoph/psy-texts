LEWF ("Philosopher") is a person with an original, complex, and deeply thoughtful personality. Like a true philosopher, they constantly reflect on life, death, the absurdity of existence, and humanity's place in the universe. They often express these existential thoughts through dark humor, joking about humanity's biggest problems. Because they combine a powerful intellect with rich emotions, the "Philosopher" can inspire people with ideas, leading them through eloquence and complete confidence in their own conclusions.

The "Philosopher's" mind works in an all-or-nothing way. They either understand a subject thoroughly or choose not to study it at all, trusting an expert instead. There is rarely any middle ground. Their statements naturally sound like final truths because they genuinely believe their reasoning is correct. For this reason, LEWF dislikes pointless debates. To them, exchanging opinions often seems unnecessary. They do not argue—they state what they believe to be facts. Once the "Philosopher" has expressed their conclusion, they consider the subject finished and see further debate as a waste of time.

LEWF is a natural empath and an expert at shaping emotional atmosphere. They need emotional responses from other people. They enjoy being the center of attention, inspiring an audience, and exploring other people's feelings. LEWF quickly reads the emotional state of those around them. Even when they appear distant or emotionally cool, it is usually not because they do not care. Instead, they may be observing the other person, testing their reactions, or encouraging a particular emotional response. They are especially drawn to emotionally open and sensitive people whose inner world they can understand in depth.

Inside LEWF there is a constant struggle between high ambitions and deep existential self-doubt. On one hand, they want to prove their importance, become a leader, and be the very best. To hide their inner uncertainty, the "Philosopher" often creates the image of an intelligent, successful, and impressive person. Because of this, many LEWFs are drawn toward art, writing, education, or public life. On the other hand, their standards for themselves are incredibly high. If a goal seems impossible, they may fall into a painful crisis of feeling unfulfilled. For LEWF, losing face in front of society can feel like a psychological death.

Most of their energy is spent generating ideas, processing emotions, and pursuing meaningful goals. Because of this, the material world becomes secondary. They are naturally undemanding. They can easily change eating habits or daily routines and usually benefit from having a more practical partner who serves as their physical anchor. However, their willingness to follow someone else's lead in physical matters has clear limits because of sensitive Third Will. The "Philosopher" is happy to adapt to another person's lifestyle—but only if decisions are made together. If practical matters are forced on them without discussion, they immediately switch into stubborn resistance and quiet sabotage.

Conclusion: The "Philosopher" presents themselves as an unshakable intellectual with natural charisma. They are ready to pursue great goals and inspire others to follow. The price they often pay is ignoring their own physical needs and constantly struggling to become the ideal version of themselves.
