In friendship and love, WELF (“Star”) is loyal, deep, but mentally difficult. The mix of royal First Will and sensitive Third Logic makes them both strong protectors and people who need gentle treatment.

Friendship

WELF is both a mentor-friend and a therapist-friend. They rarely have a huge circle of close friends, but the people they do choose are usually very special.

  • The Perfect Listener: Because of Second Emotion, people can always come to WELF when they need emotional support. WELF will not give cold, logical advice. They will honestly share the pain, calm the person down, and help them feel stronger again.
  • Defender and Protector: When a friend is hurt, First Will turns on. WELF will fight for their people, use all available contacts, and use their authority to solve the friend’s problem.
  • Intellectual Standards: WELF will not become close friends with people they see as shallow or foolish. They need deep meaning in friendly conversations. At the same time, they may become sharp or sarcastic during arguments because they are afraid of looking unskilled or uninformed.
  • Little Interest in Daily Comfort: WELF is the kind of friend who may arrive with a sleeping bag, sleep on the floor, and honestly fail to notice that the house is under repair or that there is no food.

Love and Marriage

In love, WELF is not looking for simple everyday living together. They want a deep bond of souls and shared large goals.

  • Leadership and Protection: In relationships, WELF often takes the leading role. They choose the direction of the relationship, make important decisions, and accept responsibility for the partner. Being with them can feel warm and safe, like standing behind a strong wall.
  • Emotional Romance: They know how to court someone beautifully, understand the partner’s mood, and create a deep romantic atmosphere. WELF does not usually create wild emotional scenes. Their love is shown through meaningful gestures, emotional beauty, and honest empathy.
  • Expecting Practical Care: WELF does not want to deal with repairs, cooking, or choosing furniture. They feel happiest when the partner gently takes care of the material side of life and surrounds them with quiet comfort.
  • Need for Intellectual Praise: In a relationship, WELF strongly needs a partner who truly respects their intelligence, gently calms their doubts, and avoids harsh criticism of their ideas or conclusions.

Main Relationship Challenges

  • Too Much Control: Because of First Will, WELF may slowly begin to control the partner’s whole life without noticing it. They may force their own decisions and weaken the partner’s personal freedom and initiative.
  • Defensive Aggression in Arguments: When the partner criticizes their ideas, the “Star” protects sensitive Third Logic. They may react with sharp sarcasm, irony, or counterattacks.
  • Burnout From Caring: Because of strong Second Emotion, WELF may become too deeply involved in the partner’s problems. They may lose themselves in the other person’s feelings and drain their own emotional energy.
  • Practical Immaturity: Because of Fourth Physics, WELF may ignore cleaning, household duties, and money matters. They may place all routine responsibilities on the partner, making that person feel tired and overburdened.

Compatibility With Other Types

FLEW (“Peacemaker”)—Balanced Relationship. This is a deeply harmonious relationship where both partners naturally support each other in every area. The practical and down-to-earth “Peacemaker” handles daily life, comfort, and finances. With confident Second Logic, the “Peacemaker” gently calms the “Star’s” intellectual doubts. In return, the “Star” brings a bright but safe emotional world into the relationship, takes the role of a confident leader, and protects the partner from life’s difficulties.

WLEF (“Reformer”)—Growth-Oriented Relationship. This is a deep relationship where the partners become ideal therapists for each other. The confident and flexible Second Logic of the “Reformer” gently and patiently helps the “Star” with doubts about intelligence. In return, the “Star’s” safe Second Emotion warms the partner’s closed and sensitive Third Emotion, helping them experience feelings openly and without fear. The main problem is that both partners have Fourth Physics. Because of this, their shared life may become impractical and chaotic in household and money matters.

LFWE (“Strategist”)—Dramatic Relationship. This is an attractive but exhausting relationship full of tension and painful attacks on pride. The “Strategist’s” dominant Logic feels like harsh intellectual pressure and criticism to the “Star.” At the same time, the “Strategist’s” demands for practical living clash with the “Star’s” simple and careless attitude toward daily comfort. In response, the “Star” begins to push with strong Will. The relationship can then turn into a constant fight for power, authority, and the right to decide what is correct.
