Strengths:

  • Natural Leadership: They know how to lead people, take responsibility, and calmly solve difficult problems.
  • Iron Determination: They have an unbreakable inner core and continue working until they reach major goals.
  • Emotional Intelligence: They understand people well, shape the emotional atmosphere, and motivate others.
  • Empathy and Diplomacy: They know how to listen, support people, and find the right emotional approach to almost anyone.
  • Low Need for Comfort: They can live in simple conditions and give up comfort for an important goal.

Weaknesses:

  • Authoritarian Behavior: They can become too controlling, self-focused, and demanding while ignoring other people’s wishes.
  • Easily Hurt Pride: They are highly sensitive to criticism of their intelligence, logic, ideas, and authority.
  • Love of Arguments: They may argue endlessly and use sharp words simply to defend their pride.
  • Ignoring the Body: They may neglect their health and fail to notice tiredness, hunger, or signs of illness.
  • Dependence in Everyday Life: They may struggle with routine finances and household tasks and need another person to manage these areas.
