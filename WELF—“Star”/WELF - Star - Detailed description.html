For WELF (“Star”), being the center of attention is as natural as breathing. With their magnetic charisma, they remain completely true to themselves and never reshape their personality to meet other people's expectations. “Stars” are incredibly stubborn. It is almost impossible to force them off the path they have chosen or impose another person's will upon them. They never suffer from impostor syndrome and never question their right to lead. Their inner confidence and infectious enthusiasm have an almost hypnotic effect on people, naturally inspiring others to follow them.

The world of art is where this type feels completely at home. WELF are creators with an endless creative drive, living by the motto: “Not a day without creating something.” Whether through poetry, music, painting, or another art form, they constantly generate new ideas, boldly experiment with difficult techniques, and discover striking new ways to express themselves. Their creativity is remarkably free from inhibition or self-censorship.

Despite their regal presence, the “Star” possesses profound empathy. They sincerely absorb other people's pain and rarely feel satisfied until they have helped solve the problem. This naturally attracts people to them. Yet no one can truly possess a WELF. Their vision is simply too broad. Rather than belonging to one individual, they feel devoted to humanity as a whole, often dedicating themselves to ambitious missions with wide-reaching impact.

In return, WELF searches for rare individuals capable of understanding their complex personality, decoding their hidden motives, and expanding their view of the world. They enjoy talking about themselves and often turn stories from their own lives into fascinating, emotionally rich performances. They have a particular fascination with mysterious, emotionally guarded people carrying hidden emotional wounds. WELF often feels an irresistible desire to understand, liberate, and heal such people—even when those people are not asking for it.

The inner world of the “Star” is driven by an endless hunger for knowledge. They experience genuine—even almost spiritual—awe before elegant scientific theories and enjoy spending time among highly intellectual people. For them, debate is an exciting duel that gives them energy. Interestingly, WELF often undervalues the talents that come naturally. Even when they are gifted speakers, writers, artists, or linguists, they deliberately challenge themselves with the most demanding scientific or technical subjects. For them, this is a way to overcome themselves, prove the strength of their mind, and conquer peaks that once seemed impossible.

Material life and everyday comfort rarely concern the “Star,” yet they have a remarkably distinctive relationship with style and appearance. WELF develops a unique personal aesthetic. Sometimes it consists of combinations that would seem bizarre or even ridiculous on anyone else. Yet their regal confidence and complete self-assurance transform apparent chaos into high fashion or conceptual art. Their stubbornness regarding personal image knows almost no compromise. A WELF may happily go on a difficult hiking trip wearing a long skirt and high heels simply because staying true to their chosen image matters far more than ordinary physical comfort.
