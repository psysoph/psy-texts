1st Will (1W): “Ruler”

The first function forms the core of the WELF personality.

  • Sense of Personal Right: WELF has a natural sense of dignity and authority. They do not need to prove that they have the right to lead. They already feel sure of it.
  • Determination: When WELF wants something, they will achieve it no matter what. Other people’s wishes and possible compromises matter little to them because the first function is focused on the self.
  • Responsibility: They easily take responsibility for difficult decisions, large projects, and the lives of other people. During a crisis, they act calmly and confidently.

2nd Emotion (2E): “Actor”

The second function is an area of comfort, creativity, and active contact with the world.

  • Managing the Atmosphere: WELF understands the emotional state of other people very well. They know how to improve the mood, calm someone down, or inspire people to act.
  • Artistic Talent: They often have expressive faces, beautiful speech, and a strong sense of style. They enjoy art, poetry, and theater. Their emotions usually fit the situation, change easily, and do not turn into wild emotional scenes.
  • Empathy: They are emotional givers. They are ready to listen to other people’s feelings and offer honest emotional support without forcing their own emotions onto anyone.

3rd Logic (3L): “Skeptic”

The third function is the main sensitive area and the source of constant doubt for WELF.

  • Intellectual Insecurity: They react painfully to criticism of their intelligence, ideas, or conclusions. It is difficult for them to admit that they do not know something.
  • Arguing for the Sake of Arguing: They may enter debates not to find the truth, but to prove that they are right or to test the other person. They often use irony and sarcasm in arguments.
  • Hunger for Knowledge: At the same time, Logic is very interesting to them. WELF wants a good education and enjoys difficult ideas but often wonders, "Am I smart enough?” Praise for their intelligence from respected people is very important to them.

4th Physics (4F): “Ascetic”

The fourth function is the lowest priority and receives only the energy that is left over.

  • Little Interest in Daily Comfort: WELF does not care much about luxury, fancy food, or extra comfort. They may ignore signals from the body for a long time, including tiredness, hunger, or mild illness.
  • Following Others in Material Matters: In questions of cleaning, money, health, and everyday life, they easily trust another person’s opinion. They feel comfortable when someone else takes care of the practical side of life.
  • Asceticism: They can easily live in simple or uncomfortable conditions when their great goal requires it through 1W.

Overall Psychological Portrait

WELF (“Star”) is a charismatic and proud leader who combines royal Will with strong emotional intelligence. They skillfully inspire people and take responsibility but often stay mentally guarded because their pride is easily hurt. They are very undemanding in daily life and live for large goals and higher meaning.
