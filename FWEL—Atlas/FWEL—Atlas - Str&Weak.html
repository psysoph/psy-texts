Strengths:

  • Material Stability: Excellent with money, values comfort, and knows how to build a comfortable life.
  • Reliability and Calm Under Pressure: A true "rock" that people can depend on during difficult times.
  • Balanced Leadership: Confident without controlling others and always respects personal boundaries.
  • Diplomacy: Knows how to listen, cooperate, and find fair compromises without unnecessary drama.
  • Practical Thinking: Focuses on real actions and real results instead of empty talk.

Weaknesses:

  • Emotionally Reserved: May seem cold, quiet, or distant because expressing feelings openly is difficult.
  • Sensitive to Criticism: Takes criticism, rude behavior, or fake emotions very personally.
  • Possessiveness: Can become overly protective, controlling, or jealous of loved ones and personal belongings.
  • Saving Too Much: Fear of financial problems can make them overly careful with money or even stingy.
  • Avoids Intellectual Debates: Can be vulnerable to complicated logical manipulation and quickly gets tired of abstract discussions.
