Strengths:

  • Emotional magnetism: Deeply understands feelings, sincerely cares about others, and naturally inspires people.
  • Gentle leadership: Encourages people without being controlling and always respects personal boundaries.
  • Conflict resolution: A natural peacemaker who easily helps people find compromise and rebuild relationships.
  • Creative talent: Rich imagination, strong sense of beauty, and natural ability to express themselves.
  • Emotional support: Quickly notices when someone is struggling and knows how to help them believe in themselves again.

Weaknesses:

  • Practical and financial insecurity: Often struggles with everyday responsibilities, money, and practical problems.
  • Sensitive to criticism: Takes criticism of appearance, health, sexuality, or personal taste very personally.
  • Easily influenced by authority: May accept logical arguments or expert opinions without checking whether they are actually true.
  • Emotional ups and downs: Can become overly dramatic, emotionally overwhelmed, or moody.
  • Ignoring logic: During strong emotional moments, they may make decisions that ignore common sense and clear facts.
