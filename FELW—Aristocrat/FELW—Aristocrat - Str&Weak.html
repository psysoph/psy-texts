Strengths:

  • Creates comfort: Excellent at making homes cozy, beautiful, and financially stable.
  • Emotional intelligence: Easily understands other people's feelings, shows empathy, and calms conflicts.
  • Flexible diplomacy: Easily charms people, connects with almost anyone, and inspires others.
  • Love of life: Has strong physical charm, appreciates beauty, and truly enjoys the present moment.
  • Cooperative nature: Easy to work with and does not fight for leadership or power.

Weaknesses:

  • Sensitive mind: Takes criticism of their ideas very personally and often argues because they doubt themselves.
  • Material selfishness: Can become overly possessive, jealous of their belongings, and protective of anything they own.
  • Passive and indecisive: Struggles with major life decisions, hesitates for a long time, and often waits for someone else to decide.
  • Depends on strong leaders: Can easily fall under the influence of confident people or manipulators, giving away responsibility.
  • Hides true opinions: Afraid of looking foolish, they often hide what they really think behind jokes or emotional reactions.
