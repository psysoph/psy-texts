1st Physics (1F): "Owner"

First Physics makes FELW completely down-to-earth. They see the world through comfort, physical feelings, and ownership.

  • View of the material world: Physical comfort is extremely important to FELW. They value good food, beautiful clothes, financial stability, and a comfortable life. They are naturally possessive, know how to save money, earn money, and create a beautiful living space.
  • Body and sex: They have strong physical energy and natural charm. They experience the world through touch. Sex, physical closeness, and bodily pleasure are some of the most important parts of life for them.
  • "Hammer" in action: When it comes to comfort and material things, FELW are very firm. They trust their own taste and will not accept someone rearranging their furniture or telling them how to dress.

2nd Emotion (2E): "Actor"

Second Emotion is FELW's main way of connecting with people. Unlike First Emotion, which naturally overflows, Second Emotion is gentle, flexible, and built for conversation.

  • Creating the mood: FELW are excellent actors, emotionally skilled people, and natural diplomats. They quickly notice another person's mood and easily adjust to it. They can cheer up someone who is sad or calm someone who is angry.
  • Beauty and art: They express the tastes of their First Physics (1F) through the creativity of their Second Emotion (2E). They love art, theater, and music, and many have natural artistic or acting talent.
  • Emotional generosity: They are not afraid to show feelings. They openly care about other people's emotions and know how to support them without getting lost in someone else's emotional drama.

3rd Logic (3L): "Skeptic"

Third Logic is the main area of insecurity, doubt, and hidden ambition for FELW. They want to appear smart, but they often question their own intelligence.

  • Fear of criticism: FELW take it very hard when someone calls them stupid, illogical, or incompetent. Cold intellectual criticism feels like a personal attack.
  • Thinking style: They like to examine details, debate ideas, and search for hidden meaning. They often get caught in endless doubts, check facts many times, and ask others for solid proof.
  • Defense: If an argument reaches a dead end, FELW may use their Second Emotion by turning everything into a joke or making fun of the other person, or they may use First Physics by showing material confidence or superiority.

4th Will (4W): "Follower"

Fourth Will makes FELW very easygoing when it comes to big life goals and major decisions. They have little desire to rule the world (unless money is involved) and usually let others take the lead.

  • Following others: In big life decisions, FELW usually prefer someone else to make the final choice. They easily adapt to another person's plans as long as their physical comfort (1F) is not damaged.
  • Avoiding responsibility: They do not enjoy leading people or carrying responsibility for other people's lives. FELW gladly let a confident partner take charge.
  • Trust: They easily trust people who clearly know what they want and are willing to guide and protect them.

Overall Personality

FELW ("Aristocrat") is a charming lover of comfort who stands firmly on the ground and naturally turns the world around them into a place full of beauty, comfort, and security. Thanks to their flexible emotional intelligence, they easily win people over, calm conflicts, and act as kind diplomats in everyday life. At the same time, they are deeply sensitive inside, often doubt their own thinking, and prefer to stay away from power struggles, letting others take the leading role.
