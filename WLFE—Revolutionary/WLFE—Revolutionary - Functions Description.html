1st Will (1W): "Ruler"

The First Will is strong, dominant, and completely confident.

  • Complete independence: WLFE knows what they want from an early age. They do not need advice, approval, or motivation from other people.
  • Leadership and control: They are born to lead. Their wishes become the rules. WLFE truly believes they know the best way to do things, so they naturally take charge of teams, projects, or even entire countries.
  • Determination: Problems do not scare them—they make them even more determined. WLFE pushes straight toward the goal and uses the people around them as resources. They may look calm on the outside, but inside their Will is impossible to break.

2nd Logic (2L): "Mentor"

The Second Logic is the place of confidence, discussion, creativity, and flexible thinking.

  • A tool for the Will: Logic is WLFE's main weapon. They use facts, ideas, and arguments to support their decisions.
  • Love of debate: Unlike First Logic, which simply states facts as absolute truth, WLFE enjoys debating, explaining, proving ideas, and breaking complicated topics into simple parts. They do this naturally, without anger or emotional pressure.
  • Teaching others: WLFEs often become excellent teachers, lecturers, and leaders of ideas. They can explain even the most difficult systems in simple words. Other people often respect their intelligence.

3rd Physics (3F): "Fusspot"

The Third Physics is the biggest area of insecurity, hidden ambition, and vulnerability.

  • Difficult relationship with the body: WLFE often doubts their appearance, health, or physical strength. They may swing between extremes—pushing themselves with strict diets and hard workouts, then completely ignoring their body's needs.
  • Worry about money: Money, comfort, and everyday life often create inner stress. They fear becoming poor and dislike depending on other people financially, yet they also find it difficult to organize everyday life.
  • Sensitivity and possessiveness: They are often very sensitive to smells, touch, and physical comfort. They may also become very protective of their belongings or their romantic partner.

4th Emotion (4E): "Spectator"

The Fourth Emotion is the area where they spend the least energy.

  • Emotional distance: WLFE often seems tough or emotionally cold. Other people's tears, excitement, or emotional scenes usually annoy them or leave them feeling nothing. They neither enjoy nor naturally know how to handle other people's emotions gently.
  • Following the emotional atmosphere: Because their own emotions stay quiet, they naturally match the mood around them. If everyone is happy, they relax a little. If everyone is serious, they become serious too. They easily absorb a positive emotional atmosphere, as long as it is not too overwhelming.
  • Honesty in art: WLFE may enjoy music, movies, or other art, but they usually understand it through Logic or through deep personal feelings that they never openly show.

Overall Psychological Portrait

WLFE ("Revolutionary") is a natural strategist and a charismatic leader with an unbreakable Will and a powerful analytical mind. They stay calm while making difficult decisions, quickly bring order to chaos, and direct every available resource toward their biggest goals. Behind their strong and solid image, however, there is often emotional distance toward other people and deep hidden sensitivity about health, comfort, and everyday life.
