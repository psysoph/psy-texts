Strengths:

  • Leadership and responsibility: Comfortably leads other people, makes difficult decisions, and accepts responsibility for the results.
  • Determination: Has an unbreakable Will, clearly sees their goals, and keeps moving forward no matter what.
  • Powerful and flexible mind: An excellent speaker and debater who can logically support almost any idea and convince almost anyone.
  • System thinking: Quickly brings order to chaos and creates practical systems, rules, instructions, and ideas.
  • Calm under pressure: Does not panic during crises. Stays calm and thinks clearly even in difficult situations.

Weaknesses:

  • Controlling and strict: Can become overly controlling, suppress other people's ideas, and rarely likes compromise.
  • Emotionally distant: May seem cold, uncaring, or unaware of other people's feelings, often ignoring emotional needs.
  • Sensitive about everyday life: Has hidden insecurities about health and appearance and may struggle to build a comfortable daily life.
  • Worries about money: Can become overly concerned about finances, be very sensitive to physical comfort, and become possessive.
  • Using people: May become a cold manipulator who starts seeing other people only as tools for reaching personal goals.
