WLFE is best suited for high-level leadership, large system reforms, and work with big ideas. They need to be at the top of the hierarchy, where they can put major plans into action and lead people without being distracted by other people's emotions or everyday routine.

Good Career Choices

  • Senior Executive/Top Manager—using their strong Will to lead large groups of people.
  • Politician/Government Leader—creating major reforms and leading on a national or global level.
  • Crisis Manager—staying calm while making difficult decisions during chaos.
  • Methodologist/Professor—organizing knowledge into clear systems and teaching others.
  • Serial Entrepreneur—building businesses with strong systems without getting stuck in daily routine.

Poor Career Choices

  • Psychologist/Social Worker—requires strong empathy, which is difficult because of emotional distance.
  • Actor/Entertainer/Event Host—requires constant emotional expression, which does not come naturally.
  • Interior Designer/Hotel Manager—requires a deep understanding of other people's comfort, which is a weak area.
  • Regular Employee/Secretary—constantly following someone else's orders slowly wears them down.
  • Doctor/Nurse—difficult because of sensitivity to physical discomfort and the need for emotional compassion.
