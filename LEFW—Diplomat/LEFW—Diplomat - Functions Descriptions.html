First Logic (1L): "Thinker"

First Logic is confident, strong, and self-sufficient.

  • Own opinion comes first. LEFW has a strong inner system of beliefs. They trust their own intelligence and do not need other people to approve of their ideas.
  • Result-focused. LEFW cares about reaching a final conclusion, not arguing forever. Once they have made up their mind, changing it is very difficult.
  • Straight to the point. They speak clearly and often express ideas as short statements or memorable quotes. They do not like spending time explaining things they believe are already obvious.

Second Emotion (2E): "Actor"

Second Emotion is the area of creativity, ease, and healthy interaction with the world.

  • Emotionally aware. LEFW easily understands other people's feelings, shows empathy, and offers gentle support. They never mock someone's emotions in a cruel way.
  • Creates the right atmosphere. They have natural acting talent. They can match almost any group mood—making sad people laugh or calming people who are upset.
  • Love of art. Many express themselves through poetry, music, theater, or writing. For them, emotions are a safe and enjoyable place for creativity.

Third Physics (3F): "Fusspot"

Third Physics is the main area of insecurity, inner struggle, and hidden ambition.

  • Difficult relationship with the body. LEFW often worries about health and can be easily disgusted by dirt or poor hygiene. Physical pain, discomfort, or untidiness can affect them strongly.
  • Unstable attitude toward material life. Their attitude toward money, daily life, or sex can swing between completely rejecting these things ("I'm above that") and trying to control them too much. They may worry a lot about their appearance or financial situation.
  • Needs physical safety. Physical comfort and a safe environment are very important to LEFW, but creating them without anxiety is not always easy. They do best with a partner who calmly takes care of everyday practical matters.

Fourth Will (4W): "Follower"

Fourth Will means there is little natural energy for leading or making major decisions.

  • Lets others decide. LEFW does not seek power, leadership, or control over others. They feel more comfortable when someone else makes the big life decisions, such as where to live, what career path to choose, or what direction to take.
  • Goes with the flow. They are gentle and easygoing. They usually accept other people's plans as long as those plans do not go against their strong beliefs (1L) or hurt people's feelings (2E).
  • Peaceful warrior. It is difficult for LEFW to fight for material interests (4W + 3F). But if someone attacks their ideas or moral values, their First Logic shows remarkable determination and stubbornness.

General Personality Profile

LEFW (Diplomat) is a deep thinker with the heart of a poet. They have a strong personal belief system and a rare gift for understanding other people's feelings. They are gentle, polite, and peaceful people who have no desire to control others and willingly let someone else lead. At the same time, their rich inner world stands in sharp contrast to their lack of practical everyday skills and their deep sensitivity when dealing with money, comfort, appearance, or other material matters.
