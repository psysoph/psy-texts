Strengths:

  • Powerful intelligence: Able to deeply analyze information, organize ideas, and build clear systems.
  • Strong principles: Firm confidence in their own conclusions and resistance to intellectual pressure from others.
  • Respect for others: Treats everyone as an equal, without arrogance or a desire to dominate.
  • Diplomacy: Knows how to motivate people gently, find agreements, and reach fair compromises.
  • Simple needs: Easily lives in modest conditions, does not depend on luxury, and is not obsessed with money.

Weaknesses:

  • Stubbornness: Very slow to change opinions, even when proven wrong, because their beliefs are deeply rooted.
  • Emotional distance: Often appears cold, quiet, or withdrawn and may struggle to make the first emotional connection.
  • Sensitive to criticism: Strong emotional outbursts, fake behavior, or people making fun of their feelings hurt them deeply.
  • Poor practical skills: May neglect health, household tasks, or finances if there is no reliable partner nearby.
  • Slow to act: Because of low interest in physical work, they may delay practical tasks and real-world action for a long time.
