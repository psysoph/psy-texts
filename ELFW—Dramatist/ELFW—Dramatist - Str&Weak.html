Strengths:

  • Empathy and Sensitivity: They deeply understand beauty, art, and the emotions of other people.
  • Flexible Mind: They are excellent conversation partners who can listen, understand arguments, and explain difficult things clearly.
  • Creative Potential: They have a rich imagination and a unique sense of art and beauty.
  • Diplomacy: They rarely act with open aggression and can easily make fair compromises during discussions.

Weaknesses:

  • Emotional Instability: They may experience sudden mood changes, emotional demands, and a tendency to make problems seem bigger than they are.
  • Indecision: Important choices are difficult for them. They may lose confidence when facing obstacles and often fear responsibility.
  • Practical Vulnerability: They may worry too much about health, feel strong disgust toward dirt or discomfort, and struggle with money or everyday tasks.
  • Easily Influenced: Because they find it difficult to defend their boundaries and say no, they can easily fall under another person's influence.
