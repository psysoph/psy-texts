1st Emotion (1E): "Romantic"

For ELFW, emotions are the main way of understanding life. Their whole world is shaped by feelings, moods, and inner experiences.

  • Strong Inner Feelings: The emotions of ELFW are powerful, deep, and often dramatic. They may go through strong emotional storms even when they look calm on the outside.
  • Self-Expression: Art is extremely important to Dramatist. Music, poetry, painting, and movies help them understand and express their feelings. They often have a natural sense of beauty and style.
  • Feelings Come First: ELFW does not change their mood to match other people. If they feel sad, they will not pretend to be cheerful just to please the group. They show their feelings as they are. This means their emotions are often more like a monologue than a dialogue.

2nd Logic (2L): "Mentor"

Logic is the main tool ELFW uses to communicate with the world and soften the power of First Emotion.

  • Love of Discussion: Unlike their emotions, their thinking is open to dialogue. They enjoy reasoning, debating, searching for truth, and discussing ideas. They are often interested in theories, concepts, and philosophy.
  • Flexible Mind: Dramatist can easily change arguments, look for compromise, and listen calmly to another person's point of view. They do not present their opinion as the only possible truth.
  • Intellectual Help: ELFW can be an excellent teacher. They know how to explain even a difficult subject patiently and in a simple way.

3rd Physics (3F): "Fusspot"

This is the main area of insecurity, fear, and sensitivity for ELFW. The physical world, including everyday life, the body, money, and sex, often causes inner stress.

  • Health Anxiety: ELFW is very sensitive to signals from the body. They may worry too much about illness, dirt, pain, or physical discomfort.
  • Everyday Life and Money: Comfort matters greatly to Dramatist, but creating it can be difficult. At one time, they may chase perfect order and careful self-care. At another, they may fall into strong self-denial and live with very little. They often feel unsure about money and may fear poverty, unfair prices, or being cheated.
  • Touch and Physical Details: They pay close attention to clothing, food quality, and touch. Rough physical contact or criticism of their appearance can hurt them deeply.

4th Will (4W): "Follower"

Will is the least important area for ELFW. Holding firm boundaries and making difficult decisions alone can be very hard for them.

  • Easily Led: ELFW finds it difficult to say no. It often feels easier to give in, follow someone else's plan, or obey a strong leader than to spend energy fighting.
  • Looking for a Protector: Deep inside, they often search for someone with strong Will who can take responsibility, guide them, and offer protection.
  • Going With the Flow: They may trust fate or believe that problems will solve themselves. Starting a large project alone can feel extremely stressful.

Overall Psychological Portrait

ELFW ("Dramatist") is a sensitive idealist with a rich inner world. They see life through deep feelings, beauty, and art. They have a strong and flexible mind and a gift for calm, respectful discussion. However, they often struggle with the harsh side of real life. Because they feel unsure in practical matters and have difficulty making firm decisions, they often look for a strong protector who can shield their sensitive inner world from daily problems and outside pressure.
