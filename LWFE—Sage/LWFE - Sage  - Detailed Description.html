The LWFE ("Sage") type is the image of a calm and thoughtful strategist whose cool outer behavior hides a strong ability to guide people. In communication, they keep some distance and may seem emotionally closed. There is little point in expecting dramatic tears or strong displays of sympathy from them. Instead of empty words, they will always offer clear and useful tools that can actually solve the problem. Even though LWFE is naturally private, they do not want to live alone. They need to stay close to important events and gently guide other people with calm authority. Because of this, they often become the quiet leader of their group.

Their own emotional restraint makes them interested in bright and expressive people. Open displays of emotion do not frighten the “Sage.” Instead, they often find them fascinating. The “Sage” understands the limits of their own abilities very well. Because of this, they carefully build a network of strong professionals from many different fields. Thinking about the future, they gladly invest time and resources in talented beginners. Over time, these people may become loyal allies and a reliable source of support. At the same time, the “Sage” is very practical and strict. People who break their trust or deeply disappoint them are removed from their life forever and without unnecessary emotional scenes.

The material world of LWFE is carefully organized.

They usually have clear daily habits and food routines. Any attempt to disturb these habits may cause a strong negative reaction. When they have enough resources, they can be very generous with people they love. They are also active and capable of gently encouraging even very passive people to take action.

Their view of the world is firm and clear, but they do not force it on anyone. The “Sage” honestly respects every person’s right to have a different opinion. They believe that ideas should have practical value instead of becoming reasons for pointless fights about beliefs.
