1st Will (1W): "Ruler"

The first function is the core of the WEFL personality. Here, Will is strong, solid, and centered on the person's own goals.

  • Determination: If a WEFL wants something, they will move straight toward it. Other people's doubts, rules, or limits will not stop them.
  • Leadership and responsibility: The "Messiah" has great charisma and strong confidence in their right to lead their own life—and often the lives of other people as well. Without even thinking about it, they take control of situations and people around them, often becoming a strong unofficial leader.
  • View of other people: "There is my opinion—and the wrong opinion." WEFL finds it hard to compromise when it comes to goals and personal wishes. They do not negotiate their goals—they set them.
  • 2nd Emotion (2E): "Actor"
  • For WEFL, Emotion is a tool for working with people and the world. This is an area where they feel free and confident.
  • Empathy: WEFL easily understands how other people feel. They know how to comfort, support, and inspire others.
  • Controlling the atmosphere: They can quickly lift the mood of a group or calm everyone down when emotions get too strong. Their emotions are flexible, natural, and fit the situation.
  • Self-expression: WEFL is not afraid to show feelings. Many actors, writers, speakers, and religious leaders belong to this type. Their strength and firmness (from 1W) work surprisingly well together with kindness, warmth, and emotional openness.

3rd Physics (3F): "Fusspot"

This is the weakest and most unstable part of WEFL. It creates an inner struggle between high dreams and everyday physical needs.

  • Body insecurity: WEFL often feels insecure about their looks or body weight. They are very sensitive to criticism about sex, health, or appearance, so they deeply need a kind, gentle, and careful partner.
  • Chaos in everyday life and money: WEFL often struggles to keep balance in money and daily life. They may swing between strict self-denial and impulsive spending, and they often have trouble keeping things organized.
  • Destructive work habits: Because of their powerful Will, the "Messiah" often ignores tiredness. They work until they are exhausted, which can lead to stress-related health problems and damage to their body.

4th Logic (4L): "Disciple"

Logic is the last function, so WEFL has little interest in long debates that exist only for the sake of arguing.

  • Trust in experts: WEFL does not enjoy building complicated systems of ideas. They would rather use a ready-made logical system created by someone they trust.
  • Simple thinking: They like information that is short, clear, and useful. Long abstract discussions quickly make them tired.
  • Easy to convince: If arguments sound confident and do not go against their main goals (1W), WEFL will easily accept another person's opinion and change their mind on a subject.

Overall Psychological Portrait

The WEFL ("Messiah") is a charismatic and generous leader with a powerful inner core and deep empathy. They naturally take responsibility for other people, confidently lead others, and inspire them through both their actions and their words. Even though they seem very strong on the outside, they are sensitive when it comes to the physical side of life. They deeply need comfort, care for their body, and sincere acceptance from the people close to them.
