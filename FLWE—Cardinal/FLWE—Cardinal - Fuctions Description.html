1st Physics (1F): "Owner"

The first position of Physics makes FLWE the unquestioned master of the material world.

  • Ownership and Comfort: Money, property, quality food, physical comfort, and sexuality are extremely important to them. They know exactly what they want from the physical world and pursue it confidently.
  • Self-Focused Physical Needs: FLWE organizes everyday life primarily according to their own preferences. They rarely ask others how they should dress, what they should eat, or how they should spend their money.
  • Work Ethic: They possess tremendous physical energy and usually enjoy earning money. For them, financial security represents the clearest form of safety and success.

2nd Logic (2L): "Mentor"

The second position makes Logic the primary tool for communication and cooperation.

  • Love of Discussion: FLWE enjoys debating, analyzing, and exchanging ideas—not necessarily to discover absolute truth, but because they genuinely enjoy intellectual conversation. They are excellent listeners who appreciate well-reasoned arguments.
  • Flexible Thinking: They are not dogmatic. When presented with strong evidence and logical reasoning, they are willing to revise their opinions.
  • Practical Intelligence: Their strong logical ability usually serves First Physics. They naturally apply their thinking to earning money, improving efficiency, optimizing business processes, and understanding laws, administration, and practical systems.

3rd Will (3W): "Rebel"

The third position is the main source of insecurity, internal conflict, and personal growth for the "Cardinal."

  • Status and Recognition: FLWE is extremely sensitive about authority, hierarchy, and social standing. They fear being underestimated, controlled, or denied respect.
  • Hidden Leadership: Rather than openly demanding power, they often prefer the role of the "gray cardinal"—influencing events quietly through negotiation, persuasion, compromise, and strategic thinking using Second Logic.
  • Difficulty Making Major Decisions: Large life decisions are difficult for them to make alone. They constantly move between confidence and self-doubt, wondering whether they truly deserve authority. Recognition and support from others help them become much more decisive.

4th Emotion (4E): "Spectator"

The fourth position is the weakest and least active emotional area.

  • Emotional Reserve: FLWE rarely produces strong emotions naturally. Many people see them as emotionally distant, thick-skinned, or overly practical.
  • Emotional Absorption: Rather than creating emotional atmosphere themselves, they absorb the atmosphere around them. If cheerful people surround them, they become more energetic. If they spend time with pessimistic or depressed people, they often become withdrawn as well.
  • Dislike of Drama: They strongly dislike emotional outbursts, prolonged arguments, crying, and excessive drama. In these situations they usually either leave the conversation or try to redirect it toward calm, logical discussion.

Overall Psychological Portrait

FLWE ("Cardinal") represents realism, practicality, and material competence. They skillfully manage resources, create comfortable living environments, and naturally think in terms of efficiency, usefulness, and common sense. Socially they appear intelligent, diplomatic, and flexible. Behind their calm humor and composed appearance, however, lies a vulnerable sense of status and considerable emotional distance.
