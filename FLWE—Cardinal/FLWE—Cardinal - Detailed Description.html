FLWE ("Cardinal") is one of the most private and misunderstood types. Their inner nature is built from a conflict between the wish for real, visible power and a deep fear of being weak or exposed.

Their view of the world often has a dark, realistic tone and may include an interest in things that are rough, ugly, or disturbing. They are natural “gray cardinals”: people with big ambitions who choose to stay away from bright lights and public attention. FLWE can hold grudges and strongly protect their place in society. They may see another person’s success or higher status as a threat to their own position. Their movements can seem slow, heavy, and watchful, while their quiet voice makes their strong character feel even more serious.

In society, FLWE rarely looks for easy popularity. Other people may see them as unpleasant or rude because their dry cynicism and sharp humor can hit very hard. Instead of trying to win love, they may prefer to create a clear and real sense of fear. With a sharp mind and the ability to think far ahead, FLWE can skillfully build secret plans and guide events from behind the scenes. But if they gain complete power, they may show their harshest and most controlling traits. Because of this image, FLWE is sometimes called one of the most dangerous types and is often linked with tyrants and secret planners.

Even with all this darkness and control, FLWE has one surprising quality: emotions and tears cannot change their mind, but clear facts can. Second Logic makes them honest in intellectual matters. If you calmly show them, step by step, that they are wrong, without shouting or trying to defeat them personally, the “Cardinal” can admit their mistake. For them, Logic is the only fair and civilized tool through which they are willing to meet the world as an equal.

However, behind this hard armor is a very different side. The “Cardinal’s” practical greed can exist beside a strong, almost animal-like care for their own “pack.” They sharply divide the world into insiders and outsiders. FLWE is deeply suspicious and can open up only to people who are truly selfless—people whose kindness has no hidden goal. Around their own trusted people, they can relax, although their humor may still seem rough because they do not always notice how others feel.

The “Cardinal” shows strength more than anything else but secretly searches for personal safety. They are drawn to honest, peaceful, and unambitious people who will not compete with them. Only a warm and accepting partner like this can calm their difficult nature and turn FLWE’s need to prove themselves into a strong force for building and creating.
