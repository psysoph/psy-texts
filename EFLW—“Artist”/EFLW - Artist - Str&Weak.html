Strengths:

  • Emotional Sensitivity: They understand other people’s feelings well, show true empathy, and know how to inspire those around them.
  • Everyday Care: They create comfort with skill, cook well, and gently care for the health and physical needs of close people.
  • Flexibility in Communication: They easily adjust to other people’s plans, do not create fights over leadership, and often give way in arguments to keep the peace.
  • Desire to Grow: They constantly want to learn new things, read a lot, and study subjects that interest them in depth.
  • Easy to Motivate at First: They gladly support other people’s ideas, trips, and projects when they do not have to carry all the responsibility alone.

Weaknesses:

  • Easily Led and Indecisive: They struggle to make important life choices, tend to go with the flow, and can fall under another person’s influence.
  • Sensitive Pride in Arguments: They react very painfully to criticism of their intelligence. They may argue for hours over small details because they are trying to protect their pride.
  • Weak Personal Boundaries: Because of their gentle nature, they struggle to say “no.” This may allow other people to control, pressure, or take advantage of them.
  • Emotional Instability: They may express feelings too strongly or in a self-focused way, forcing other people to adjust to their mood.
  • Fast Loss of Motivation: They become excited about a new goal easily but may give up at the first serious problem when there is no strong leader nearby.
