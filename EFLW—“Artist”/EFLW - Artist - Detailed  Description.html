EFLW (“Artist”) meets the world with open, almost childlike trust. These are truly positive people who honestly love life itself. Their feelings are impossible to miss. You can see them at once in their bright eyes, lively voice, expressive face, and natural tone. Because of strong First Emotion, the "Artist" cannot and does not want to hide how they feel. They are naturally free from revenge, jealousy, and secrecy. When something excites them, they say so loudly and with joy. When something hurts or upsets them, they speak directly, without hidden meanings or games.

The material world is an endless source of beauty and inspiration for EFLW. They notice beauty everywhere, from buildings and wild landscapes to another person’s style. The “Artist” gives sincere compliments freely because they enjoy making people happy. Caring for themselves feels just as natural. They look after their appearance for their own sense of balance, not because of trends or social rules. An active lifestyle and healthy habits help them build physical energy, which they gladly use for everyday tasks and creating comfort.

Learning about the world is one of EFLW’s main values. They truly enjoy studying, and their mind can create deep, unusual, and original ideas. However, because of sensitive Third Logic, the “Artist” always carries some doubt about their own knowledge. This is why they often admire other people’s intelligence with no jealousy and may strongly praise well-educated people. They are deeply afraid of harsh intellectual pressure. At the same time, their strong emotions mean they cannot always be soft in arguments. It is hard for them to adjust to another person’s view, and they defend their own ideas openly and emotionally.

EFLW does not feel a strong need to prove anything, compete for status, or fight for a place in the world. They simply do what they love and cannot imagine living any other way. At the same time, the “Artist” is one of the least conflict-loving types. They naturally focus on the best qualities in people, so ordinary fights do not interest them. Other people also find it hard to stay angry at their open and innocent nature. Because of their Fourth Will, the one thing EFLW truly needs is for close people to show interest in their passions. Outside interest and gentle support are the fuel that helps the “Artist” finish what they started and reach greater success.
