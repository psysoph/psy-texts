1st Emotion (1E): “Romantic”

The emotional world is the main way EFLW understands life.

  • Bright Feelings: They have a very rich inner world. They deeply feel art, music, poetry, and the moods of people around them.
  • Honesty: They do not know how—and do not want to—to hide their emotions. When they are sad or happy, it can be seen on their face right away.
  • Self-Focused Feelings: They are independent in their emotional experiences. When EFLW is in a good mood, it is hard to upset them. When they are in a bad mood, other people’s attempts to cheer them up may feel annoying.
  • Creative Drive: They often find themselves in art, writing, acting, or design.

2nd Physics (2F): “Artisan”

The material world feels clear, pleasant, and safe to them.

  • Everyday Comfort: They know how to create a cozy home without showing off. They are often good cooks, enjoy massage and physical touch, and value well-made things.
  • Help Through Action: They gladly help other people improve their homes, deal with a cold, or clean up. They do it easily and without putting pressure on anyone.
  • Endurance: They have a healthy connection with their body. They can usually judge their physical strength and health in a realistic way.
  • Generosity: They easily share money and resources. They do not collect things or save money just for the sake of having more.

3rd Logic (3L): “Skeptic”

This is the most sensitive and painful area of EFLW and must be treated with care.

  • Love of Knowledge: It is very important for them to seem intelligent and truly be intelligent. They enjoy learning, collecting facts, and proving their point.
  • Fear of Looking Foolish: They are deeply afraid of looking unskilled or uninformed. Criticism of their intelligence or arguments hurts them more than almost anything else.
  • Constant Debater: Because they feel unsure of themselves, they may argue simply for the sake of arguing. They may focus on tiny mistakes in another person’s thinking to protect their pride.
  • Need for Reassurance: They need a calm conversation partner who can explain a difficult idea without shouting or pressure. They also need to hear that they understood it correctly.

4th Will (4W): “Follower”

This is the area of decisions, ambition, and responsibility that EFLW gladly shares with close people.

  • Following Others: They easily agree to other people’s plans. It is hard for them to stay focused on their own goal for a long time without outside support.
  • Peacefulness: They strongly dislike conflict, fights for power, and competition. They would rather give way than damage peace and good relationships.
  • Dependence on Their Environment: They are strongly affected by the people around them. A partner with strong Will can guide EFLW toward success but can also control and suppress them.
  • Easy to Join In: When someone else handles the planning—where to go or what to do—EFLW happily joins without many complaints.

Overall Psychological Portrait

EFLW (“Artist”) is a warm and practical romantic who lives through strong feelings and creates real comfort around them. They are easy to live with, ready for new experiences, and happy to care for the physical comfort of close people. At the same time, they are deeply afraid of criticism of their intelligence and tend to drift through life while avoiding responsibility for major decisions.
