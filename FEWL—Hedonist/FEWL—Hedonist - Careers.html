The ideal field for FEWL is working with people through the physical world and emotions. They do well in jobs where they can create real comfort, care for the body and health, give people beauty and pleasure, or manage the mood of a group. They thrive where results can be seen quickly and there is no harsh fight for power.

Good Career Choices

  • Chef/Pastry Chef/Restaurant Owner—creating amazing food and building an atmosphere of comfort.
  • Interior Designer/Decorator—changing spaces and creating real comfort through taste and style.
  • Massage Therapist/SPA Specialist/Cosmetologist—caring for the body, health, and physical relaxation of others.
  • Family Psychologist/Mediator—gently solving conflicts and creating emotional warmth.
  • Actor/Event Host/Entertainer—guiding the mood of an audience and giving people bright emotions with ease.

Poor Career Choices

  • Crisis Manager/Chief Executive Officer—requires hard pressure on people and major long-term decisions.
  • Politician/Military Officer—difficult because of the dislike of harsh competition, power struggles, and strict rank.
  • Theoretical Scientist/Nuclear Physicist—involves dry abstract analysis and little connection to real daily life.
  • Software Developer/Systems Architect—requires deep work with difficult mathematical rules, algorithms, and systems.
  • Auditor/Tax Inspector—involves heavy financial responsibility, repetitive numbers, and constant stress from checking other people's work.
