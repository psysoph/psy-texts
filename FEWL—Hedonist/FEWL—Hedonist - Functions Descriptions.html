1st Physics (1F): "Owner"

The first position of Physics makes the "Hedonist" the complete owner of the physical world. They experience life through the body, touch, tastes, and smells.

  • Body and comfort: They clearly notice even the smallest signals from their body and have a natural gift for making the space around them comfortable. They love good food, beautiful clothes, massage, and physical pleasure. They also make sure that everything around them is clean, warm, cozy, and full of food.
  • Possessiveness: They clearly know the limits of their body and property. The Hedonist is not aggressive, but they will not give away their belongings, money, or personal space without permission.
  • Self-focus in comfort: They create comfort mainly for themselves. Other people may enjoy this comfort too, but First Physics will not change its physical habits to fit someone else's wishes.

2nd Emotion (2E): "Actor"

Emotion in the second position is the "Hedonist's" main tool for dealing with people. It is flexible, strong, healthy, and open to communication.

  • Creating the mood: They have a rare gift for calming conflict and lifting people's spirits. They notice other people's emotional state very well.
  • Emotional support: They know how to listen, show care, and cheer someone up without putting too much pressure on them. People feel emotionally safe around the "Hedonist."
  • Managing emotions: They can easily match another person's mood when support is needed, but they do not lose control of their own feelings. They avoid harsh emotional pressure and dramatic behavior.

3rd Will (3W): "Rebel"

The third position is the "Hedonist's" main weak spot. It is the area of hidden insecurity, ambition, and self-doubt.

  • Difficulty choosing and need for leadership: The "Hedonist" finds it very hard to make important long-term decisions, take on strict responsibility, or set distant goals. Because of this, they deeply need a person with strong Will—either 1W or 2W—who can gently but firmly take the lead and point them in the right direction.
  • Dependence on other people's opinions: They react badly when someone criticizes their leadership, authority, or determination. They also find it hard to say no, so they often take on other people's problems and then quietly build up hurt and anger.
  • Hidden stubbornness: The "Hedonist" may seem soft and easygoing, but they can suddenly become stubborn and refuse to move if they feel someone is pushing or controlling them too harshly. This is a defense reaction of Third Will.

4th Logic (4L): "Disciple"

The fourth position means that pure theories, abstract ideas, and arguments for the sake of arguing do not interest the "Hedonist" very much.

  • Practical thinking: They look at information mainly through its real-life use. They want to know how it can help them earn money, improve health, or make the home better.
  • Trust in experts: They easily accept other people's ideas, systems, and rules if they sound clear and reasonable. They do not enjoy long debates or proving that they are right.
  • Simple explanations: They prefer difficult things to be explained in simple words, without fancy terms. If a discussion becomes too abstract or too long, the "Hedonist" quickly loses interest.

Overall Psychological Portrait

FEWL ("Hedonist") is the image of comfort, physical stability, and emotional warmth. These people are excellent at building a pleasant home, understanding other people's feelings, and calming conflicts. They create a safe, warm, and well-fed space around themselves. Even though they seem soft and caring, they react badly to pressure, often hide hurt feelings, and struggle with major responsibility and difficult analysis.
