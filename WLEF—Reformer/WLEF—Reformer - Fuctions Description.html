1st Will (1W): "Ruler"

The first position makes Will extremely strong and confident.

  • Determination and leadership: The "Reformer" always knows exactly what they want. Their goals and wishes come first. They never doubt their right to lead, make decisions, and take responsibility.
  • Independence: WLEF is extremely independent. Other people's pressure has little effect on them. They will either push back firmly or simply ignore anyone trying to control them.
  • View of people: They often judge people by their abilities and by how much they can contribute to a goal. At the same time, they can be excellent mentors who help others grow and improve.

2nd Logic (2L): "Mentor"

Logic is the main way WLEF connects with the world and puts their First Will into action.

  • Love of discussion: The "Reformer" enjoys debating, building arguments, and searching for the truth. The process of thinking is exciting to them.
  • Respect for different opinions: Unlike questions of power, where First Will takes charge, WLEF is open-minded when it comes to ideas. They are willing to listen to any argument if it makes logical sense.
  • Teaching others: They enjoy explaining difficult ideas in simple words. They like helping people think more clearly and pointing out weak spots in someone's reasoning.

3rd Emotion (3E): "Wallflower"

The third position is the main source of inner conflict, insecurity, and hidden feelings.

  • Emotionally reserved: The "Reformer" finds it difficult to openly show strong emotions like joy, excitement, or sadness. Their face and voice often seem calm, serious, or even cold.
  • Fear of fake emotions: They are very sensitive to dishonesty, fake feelings, and overacting. Loud emotional scenes or dramatic behavior make them uncomfortable and push them away.
  • Need for acceptance: Deep inside, WLEF is much more sensitive than they appear. They need a calm, safe emotional environment where people accept them without drama or pressure to "be more cheerful."

4th Physics (4F): "Ascetic"

The physical world is not a high priority for WLEF. It always comes after their bigger goals.

  • Simple needs: The "Reformer" can easily live with simple food, basic comfort, and very little luxury if they are focused on something important.
  • Lets others handle daily life: When it comes to health, clothes, cooking, or decorating a home, they are happy to trust someone else's taste or follow reliable advice.
  • Saving energy: They do not like wasting energy on unnecessary physical work or collecting money just for the sake of having more. Money is simply a tool for reaching their goals or buying books, education, and knowledge.

Overall Psychological Portrait

WLEF ("Reformer") is a natural strategic leader with an unbreakable inner core and a powerful analytical mind. They stay calm during crises, solve difficult problems with confidence, and enjoy thoughtful discussions. Although they seem emotionally reserved and live simply, WLEF has strong personal values and a deeply hidden emotional sensitivity that few people ever see.
