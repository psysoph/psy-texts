WLEF is best suited for strategic leadership, analysis, and idea-driven leadership. They need the freedom to make their own decisions and work that keeps their mind busy. Their job should not require strong emotional involvement or hard physical labor.

Good Career Choices

  • Strategic Director/Top Manager—leading large projects, managing crises, and making calm decisions under pressure.
  • Research Scientist/Analyst—building complex logical systems, finding hidden patterns, and creating new ideas.
  • Business Consultant/Auditor—reviewing business processes independently and finding logical mistakes in other people's strategies.
  • Professor/University Lecturer—leading interesting academic discussions and explaining difficult ideas in simple words.
  • Politician/Lawmaker—creating large programs, leading with ideas, and taking part in public debates.

Poor Career Choices

  • Psychotherapist/Entertainer—requires constant work with other people's emotions, strong empathy, and emotional support.
  • Technical Support Specialist/Call Center Operator—requires dealing with difficult people every day while having little freedom or initiative.
  • Regular Employee (in a strict hierarchy)—constantly following someone else's orders is difficult for their independent nature.
  • General Doctor/Nurse—requires close contact with people's physical problems and a strong focus on the body.
  • Jeweler/Chef/Craftsman—requires detailed handwork, great precision, and constant attention to physical details.
